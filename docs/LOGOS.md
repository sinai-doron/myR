# Store logos

The app shows a store's logo next to its receipts, only to identify the store. The logos are
trademarks of their owners. They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence. Each one is
here only if its source states a licence that allows redistribution. Stores without one get their
initial on a purple circle.

Not available with a licence that allows redistribution (checked 2026-10-31): Super-Pharm, Delek,
Cal, Dor Alon / Alonit and Kravitz have logos only on Wikipedia, marked non-free; no free logo was
found for Osher Ad, MAXstock, Yochananof, Tzomet Sfarim, Carrefour Israel, Jumbo, M-Teura or
Dochan. Delta Galil's free logo is the corporate mark, not the stores', so it is not used.

Adding one: prefer a source with a stated licence (Wikimedia Commons, Simple Icons). Aggregator
sites that state no licence (seeklogo and similar) are not used. Add a row here in the same commit.
